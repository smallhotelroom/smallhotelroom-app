# Privacy Policy — Small Hotel Room

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026

**Small Hotel Room** (`com.smallhotelroom.app`) is a free, open-source, offline music and songbook application developed by Small Hotel Room.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Small Hotel Room operates completely offline on your device. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or crash reports.

## 2. Local Storage & Imported Files
All user preferences and any optional SQLite (`.db`) songbook packs you choose to import are stored strictly locally on your device using standard local storage (`localStorage` and `IndexedDB`) and never leave your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Ads
The application contains no advertisements, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases. External links (such as optional links to Spotify or project websites in song metadata) open in your device's default browser or external app only when explicitly tapped by you.

## 4. Contact & Legal Notice
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact details on our official legal page at [https://smallhotelroom.com/legal](https://smallhotelroom.com/legal) or by opening an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/smallhotelroom/smallhotelroom-app/issues).
